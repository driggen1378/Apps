## Stack integration

Three docs cascade. Each has a distinct role and edit cadence:

- **Macro (this doc, .md):** big-picture strategic frame. The WHY. Slow-changing — updates only when the bet itself shifts (target market change, sequencing reversal, long-arc redefinition).
- **Micro (.md):** working memory and operational layer. The WHAT-NEXT. Workstreams, triggers, fail conditions, step-level Notes. Updates as reality changes — weekly or faster. **The micro is the source the Gantt chart in Claude Code builds from.**
- **Brand-DDC (.md, NEW in v2.6, revised v2.7):** active capture layer for Asset 1 forward-validation under A20 + A21 + A22 (revised). The HOW-DOCUMENTED. Gary Vee *document, don't create* — capture from real work (working sessions, podcast prep, dissertation reading, training, conversations), tag lightly by domain and surface, harvest into posts. Iteration matrix and kill criteria from v2.6 are gone; tags are retrospective signal tools, not pre-committed cells. Updates as captures happen. Archives when Asset 1 compounds (frame validated, merges back into macro) or when 60 days of capture + posting produces no ICP signal (frame falsifies, A20/A21/A22 update).

**Flow.** Strategic conversations refine the macro → cascade to micro → micro feeds the Gantt chart Claude Code maintains. The brand-ddc doc operates as a satellite to micro (specifically B2 / B4a workstreams) until merge-back into macro at compounding. Direction is one-way under normal operation; only major reframes flow upward from micro to macro, or from brand-ddc capture+harvest results to macro.

**Tool split.** Strategic dialogue happens in Claude chat. Operational work — Gantt visualization, task tracking, file edits — happens in Claude Code, sourced from the micro.

**Implication for working memory.** Anything produced in strategic dialogue that should survive must promote to micro (or brand-ddc, if capture-shaped) before the chat ends. Anything in micro that should be visible at task level becomes a Gantt entry in Code. Items that don't earn promotion at either layer stay as conversational artifacts only.

## Page 1 — The Bet

**Thesis.** A USAFWS-credentialed organizational design leader, post-W-2 separation (Metrea final day 2026-06-12), enters the workforce via a **70/30 portfolio**: ~70% cleared defense / training / intelligence / exercise / capture roles (where the USAFWS credential is a premium signal) plus ~30% OD-bridge titles (Workforce Transformation, Human Capital — Defense, Change Management, Operating Model, Organizational Effectiveness) where defense/training background is structurally legible. Direct OD Manager applications at top consultancies become stretch targets, not baseline. Geographic constraint: Las Vegas local or remote only (no relocation). Solo emergence remains trigger-based, not calendar-based. Long arc: a B2B2C credentialing body or productized advisory practice generating $1M/year in years 15–20.

**Cash context (revised from v2.3).** Peak liquid mid-July ~$42K (savings ~$28K + severance $6K + 30 days additional pay $8K). Burn ~$3K/mo, +$400 Jul/Aug, +$2K Nov. VA disability ~$1K/mo possible but not budgeted. Runway floor (~$12K) does not fire until mid-2027 even with zero income. **Cash compression is no longer the binding constraint.** The binding constraint is now vocational direction: choosing the right lane, building the right asset stack, and resolving dissertation scope without rushing.

### Why 70/30, not direct OD Manager (A14)

Three different credential effects, not one:
- **Premium** in cleared defense / intel / training / exercise / capture roles — USAFWS reads exactly as the buyer wants.
- **Discount** in mainstream tech OD — McChrystal/Coughlin pattern; tech buyers discount combat heritage as a sales signal.
- **Discount** for direct OD-title attempts anywhere — absence of conventional OD-title history triggers the screen. (Deloitte OD Manager 327202 auto-rejected 2026-05-XX is the first data point.)

The 70/30 split is the operational response to all three. Defense is the strongest income-protective lane because the credential is a premium there. OD-bridge titles open the OD path without colliding with the direct OD-title screen. Direct OD-Manager attempts continue only where fit is unusually strong (named referral, specific program match, unusual JD flexibility).

### Why this IS the Coughlin sequence run literally

The reference model is **Dr. Gene Coughlin**, founder of the **Resilience Building Leader Program (RBLP)**. The pattern in full:

- 20 years U.S. Marine Corps (Infantry Weapons Officer, 1987–2007) — operator credibility
- ~10 years post-military as Program Manager at SAIC, AECOM, CACI, American Red Cross (2008–2019) — B2B / federal-channel fluency
- Doctoral research at Pepperdine University on **specific practices military leaders use to build resilience** — the IP
- Founded RBLP in 2018 — productized the doctoral finding into a tiered certification (RBLP, RBLP-Coach, RBLP-Trainer)
- Pricing: $400 one-time lifetime certification fee
- Scale model: **Authorized Training Partner (ATP) network** — partners deliver the cert; Coughlin's org licenses the IP
- Validation rails: DoD credentialing program approval, ACE college credit recommendations, GI Bill funding eligibility

The user's path replicates the pattern but with the doctorate done *concurrently* with the defense-contractor seasoning, not after. This compresses the sequence by ~5–7 years if it works. Risk: trying to do codification (Asset 2) and credibility-building (Asset 1) simultaneously is harder than doing them in series.

### Why job-first beats cold-start solo at this point in the arc

Cold-start solo at 15–20 hrs/wk into a credential-discounting vertical with no warm network is the hardest possible launch path. Practitioner literature (Weiss, Fields, Consulting Success surveys) tacitly assumes either full-time devotion *or* a warm Rolodex. Neither holds. **The job is now primarily a vehicle for vocabulary, network density, and internal proof points — not a cash-survival mechanism.** Job-first acquires these as a byproduct of being paid, closing the named gaps (civilian L&D vocabulary, HRIS/LMS platforms, people analytics KPIs, HR ecosystem fluency, target-vertical network) without out-of-pocket cost.

### Why parallel LLC + side practice is deferred (revised reasoning)

LLC formation is cheap and asynchronous (~$425, 1–2 weeks of evening effort). The reason to defer is no longer cash. **It is brand-stack sequencing.** Asset 1 (podcast/YouTube) has not yet shown compounding behavior. Building Asset 3 (consulting LLC, services page, pricing) before Asset 1 compounds means scaffolding without a foundation. LLC formation resumes when Asset 1 shows clear directional pull or when a paid pilot opportunity surfaces (whichever comes first).

**The configuration constraint** (carried forward): while interviewing for full-time roles, do NOT publicly run active solo-sales outreach with offers and pricing. Positioning conflict.

### Why the dissertation is Asset 2, not parallel academic work (NEW framing in v2.4)

Workstream C was previously treated as an independent track on the academic calendar. v2.4 reframes it: **the dissertation is the structurally-funded production of Asset 2.** Whatever topic, site, and methodology are chosen become the spine of every codification asset downstream — book, framework, certification, methodology IP. The chair conversation (Micro C1) is not "pitch a topic." It is "negotiate scope for the artifact that becomes the eventual codification layer." This shifts how the topic, methodology, and site are selected.

### Dissertation scope is in resolution (A15 revised — supersedes v2.3 "debrief anchor")

The v2.3 macro locked debrief phenomenology as the dissertation anchor (A15). **v2.4 removes that lock.** The dissertation is in scope resolution. Six scopes to resolve, sequenced by UNC OLL grad-plan milestones:

| Scope | Description | Resolution window |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Topic | The researchable question | By Year 2 mid-term (comps + topic confirmation) |
| 2. Site | The organization where data is collected | Before topic confirmation; **must NOT be current employer** (Metrea contraindication) |
| 3. Meaning | Why it matters to org + scholarship | Year 2 Sem 2 |
| 4. Practice | The specific operational problem | Year 2 mid-term |
| 5. Methodological | RCA, Design Thinking, or Program Evaluation | Year 3 Sem 1 (Capstone I template) |
| 6. Instrument | Protocols, rubrics, eval frameworks | Year 3 Sem 2 (proposal defense) |

**UNC's DiP offers three methodological pathways: Root Cause Analysis, Design Thinking, Program Evaluation.** Phenomenology is not on the menu. The pathway is forced by the problem state at the chosen site, not by methodological preference: cause unknown → RCA; cause known and designing → DT; solution exists and evaluating → PE. **Selecting a methodology before site and problem are clear is a planning error.**

### Why the $1M arc still requires B2B2C credentialing (A11 unchanged, refined)

Project-based consulting math doesn't pencil for $1M/year solo — you would need 80+ engagements/year at $12K each. Impossible. The $1M paths are:

1. **Owned credentialing body with ATP royalties (Coughlin/RBLP shape)** — generic certifiable content, partner-delivered, royalty-scaled.
2. **Productized IP + speaking/licensing** — book + framework + paid keynotes. Personal-brand-shaped.
3. **Retainer-anchored advisory** at $20–30K/mo with 3–4 clients — high-margin, capacity-bounded, dies if you stop.

The job-first arc builds toward whichever of these three the wedge hypothesis (A19) eventually resolves to. The dissertation is the IP that makes any of them defensible — but only if its scope (1–6 above) is selected with this future use in mind.

### A19 — Wedge hypothesis (revised in v2.5)

The product hypothesis under exploration is **NOT** Coughlin's RBLP-shape (generic certifiable content sold via ATP network). It is closer to **Kotter International** or **BTS Group**: a methodology for org-specific work, where the methodology itself becomes the certifiable IP.

**Hypothesis statement:** Internal L&D leads, HR directors, or operating leaders can be trained in a specific methodology for designing manager development tailored to their organization's actual work — so the org becomes self-sufficient in manager development and doesn't rely on external content libraries that don't fit their context.

**Method candidates, leading wedge tightened in v2.5:**

1. ~~Debrief-as-design-framework~~ — secondary candidate; competing IP exists (Teschner / VMax, Boucousis / Afterburner).
2. ~~Authority-mapping → development-needs~~ — secondary candidate; contested by Marquet / McChrystal.
3. **Operator-interview needs extraction — LEADING WEDGE.** Structured interviewing of operators surfaces lived-experience data as training-design input. Tight convergence with A21 (podcast nucleus): the public-facing podcast is the live laboratory of the extraction methodology; the dissertation codifies it as a transferable protocol; the org-facing delivery applies it to soon-to-leave senior experts inside organizations losing institutional knowledge.

**Important distinction (carried from v2.4):** the *product methodology* (A19) and the *dissertation methodology* (A15) are not necessarily the same artifact. The dissertation is constrained to UNC's three pathways (RCA / DT / PE). The product wedge is unconstrained. They may converge (e.g., a Design Thinking DiP that produces the methodology as its design artifact, or an RCA DiP whose root-cause finding is "implicit institutional knowledge has no extraction protocol"). Resolution is part of the C1 chair conversation.

**Resolution path:** A19 is pre-product. No direct demand data exists. Validation work is Phase 2-3 (in-role, real buyer access, small pilots). A19 stays in "hypothesis" status until pilot data exists. The podcast (B2) is the early-stage laboratory — every interview that successfully extracts a transmissible lesson is a method test at N=1.

### A21 — Lessons-learned as podcast-side nucleus (NEW in v2.5)

**Claim:** The user has been running a lessons-learned-shaped through-question on the podcast (and in life) for years; the public articulation of it has been the hedge, not the practice. Stating it explicitly resolves three operational problems simultaneously: guest sourcing, guest preparation, and content cohesion.

**On-tape evidence (already running in private):**
- yfh-34 (solo): user defines the newsletter on-air as "lessons learned from either the people that I'm talking to who are far smarter than I am, or I draw from lessons from my individual life and I just curate them." Newsletter is already named for the mechanic.
- Episode 8 (solo, "no template for what best looks like"): structured as a lesson extraction from his own WIC instructor experience — "I learned that being the best means knowing what best means and having the courage to stop when you get there."
- Hilstrom interview (yfh-#) and Pistol interview (Norman Driggers solo): interview questions are decision-logic and constraint-extraction shaped, not war-story shaped. "What were the constraints, what was the decision logic" is a lessons-learned mechanic.

**The through-question, operationalized:** every guest interview routes through some version of *what did you learn doing this* — but instrumented: what was the decision, what was the constraint, what did you not see coming, what would you tell yourself ten years ago.

**Why this is uncrowded:**
- Rogan: curiosity-conversation, not lesson-extraction.
- Williamson: research-curation, not lesson-extraction.
- Jocko: lesson-extraction, but only from combat-leadership memoirs.
- Stumpf: persona-and-presence, methodology stays private.
- Hasard Lee: teaches frameworks, doesn't extract them.

The position "structured extraction of transmissible lessons from live experts across any high-experience domain" is empty in the operator-aesthetic creator space.

**Why it resolves operational problems:**
- *No reach to pull famous guests:* the bar is lived experience + reflection capacity, not fame. Recruiting shifts from network-based to category-based (training failures, business pivots, parenting under stress, recovering from injury, building from scratch).
- *Inner circle exhausted:* categories scale; relationships don't.
- *Guests don't think they're interesting:* "what did you learn from X" hands them the assignment. They know what to bring.

**Convergence with A19 (leading wedge):** A21 and A19-candidate-3 are the same mechanic at different altitudes.
