## What this doc is

A running capture of moments, lessons, reactions, and observations from real work — working sessions like the one that birthed this doc, podcast prep, dissertation reading, training, conversations, life. Capture is the point. Polishing comes later, if at all.

Gary Vee's frame: **document, don't create.** The substrate of your daily life (USAFWS instructor, doctoral student, podcaster, post-institutional transition, mind/body practitioner, sociology-and-OD nerd) is invisible to you because you live it — and not invisible to anyone outside it. Curse of knowledge is the binding obstacle. DDC bypasses it by treating the lived stuff as already-interesting, because to outsiders, it is.

This doc replaces brand-stack.md v0.1 (the 4 × 3 experimental matrix), which over-engineered the problem it was supposed to solve. The drift correction is itself documented as Capture #6 below.

## Binding constraints (the few hard rules that actually apply)

These bind every capture and every harvest. If a candidate post would violate one of these, it doesn't ship — even if the capture is strong.

1. **No wordmark lock.** "Lessons learned," "extraction," "leverage," "creation philosophy" — none of these become locked branding language until Asset 1 compounds (Macro A21 risk note). Captures use whatever language is natural; harvest polish stays loose.
2. **No paid solo-sales content while interviewing.** Position-taking, lesson-sharing, reaction posts are fine. Offers, pricing, "work with me" CTAs are out until DP1 fires or T3 conditions are met (Macro non-pursuits).
3. **Persona public, methodology private.** Document the *journey, reactions, lessons, observations*. Do NOT document the *operating protocols* behind eventual paid work — extraction methodology, OD frameworks, dissertation methodology. Stumpf mechanic. This is the one place where Gary Vee's total-broadcast DDC has to be tuned for the user's actual brand-stack architecture.
4. **Audience composition is signal, not raw counts.** At <1K subs, view counts are noise. The harvest tracker scores by who engaged (ICP-adjacent? operator-class? civilian curious?), not by how many.

## Audience filter — substrate-validator (NEW in v0.2)

This section is NOT a wordmark lock. It is a working filter that makes harvest directed instead of random. The distinction matters:

- **Wordmark lock** = locked branding language, slogan, value prop. Macro A21 risk note says don't lock yet.
- **Audience filter** = defining *who you're trying to land with* so captures can be filtered before they ship. This is encouraged, not prohibited. They are different concepts and conflating them generates random content.

**The substrate-validator audience.** You don't need to know your eventual *buyer* audience yet (that's Asset 3/4). At sub-scale, you need to know your *substrate-validator* audience — people who, when they see a capture, recognize the institutional reality you're describing. Their engagement IS the signal that the captures are landing.

**Working definition of the filter:**

> *"Would a thoughtful person who has lived inside an institution — military, elite team, doctoral program, high-craft organization, or analog — find this resonant or recognize the reality being described?"*

Not a demographic. A *quality* of audience.

**Why this filter and not another.** Direct application of USAFWS target-and-audience analysis ("you can teach anybody anything, but target-and-audience analysis is the route to good instruction"). The substrate-validator filter exploits the user's actual substrate moat — institutional lived experience — rather than competing in the open PD lane against creators with better attention infrastructure.

**Applied to seed captures (validation):**
- Capture #1 (curse-of-knowledge) → passes (anyone deep inside any expertise recognizes this)
- Capture #2 (hiding behind guests) → passes (anyone who's interviewed/created recognizes)
- Capture #3 (audience composition over counts) → passes weakly (creator-shaped, not institutional)
- Capture #4 (AI drift in positioning) → passes weakly (Meta-shaped, not institutional)
- Capture #5 (premature vs. earned simplicity) → passes (anyone who's iterated through complex problems recognizes)
- Capture #6 (the meta-capture about this session) → passes (anyone who's seen plans drift recognizes; both institutional + creator)

**Implication:** captures tagged `Meta` lean toward a creator/AI-user audience rather than the substrate-validator. They can still post but shouldn't dominate the harvest, or surface area shifts from substrate moat to crowded creator-meta lane.

## Harvest filter — the decision rule for shipping (NEW in v0.2)

When a capture is candidate-for-shipping (status `captured` → `drafted`), run it through this filter. Without an explicit rule, harvest devolves to "I felt like it" and the substrate moat doesn't compound.

| # | Filter check | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Does it violate a binding constraint (wordmark lock, paid solo-sales, methodology broadcast)? | KILL |
| 2 | Does it sound like Naval / Tim Ferriss / Williamson / generic PD? | KILL or SHARPEN with institutional specificity (USAFWS, doctoral reading, specific operator, named historical case) |
| 3 | Does it pass the substrate-validator audience filter (above)? | Pass = candidate; Fail = kill or move to a different audience filter explicitly |
| 4 | Does it make a claim outsiders would disagree with? | STRONG candidate |
| 5 | Does it cite specific institutional/operator material (USAFWS, Top Gun origin, doctoral readings, named ops, specific instructor experience)? | STRONG candidate — substrate moat |
| 6 | Does it surface a contradiction or tension in mainstream thinking? | STRONG candidate |
| 7 | Is it tip-shaped, listicle-shaped, or quote-card-shaped? | KILL at sub-scale — too crowded, no moat |

**Two or more STRONG hits + zero kills = ship.** One strong hit + zero kills = ship with light sharpening. Anything else = sharpen or kill.

Filter applies at *harvest*, not at *capture*. Captures are raw — capture everything. Filtering is downstream.

## Light tagging schema

When capturing, tag each entry by domain and likely surface. Tags are retrospective signal tools, not pre-commitments.

**Domain tags:**
- `Mil/Intel/Strat` — military, intelligence, strategy, institutional pedagogy
- `OD/Soc` — organizational design, sociology, group dynamics, institutional knowledge
- `Human-Opt` — mind/body/spirit, practices, training, recovery
- `Leadership/PD` — leadership, decision-making, personal development
- `Meta` — content, creation process, AI use, branding itself

**Surface tags:**
- `YT-long` — podcast episode hook or solo segment
- `YT-short` — 30–90s extraction
- `LI-post` — 150–300 word native LinkedIn post
- `LI-carousel` — 6–10 page carousel
- `Newsletter` — Substack issue inclusion
- `Multiple` — works across surfaces with format adaptation

**Status:** `captured` → `drafted` → `posted` → `killed` (capture didn't survive review)

### 2026-05-22 · Workstream A resume-build + LinkedIn audit session (4 captures)

Extracted from the session that built the dual-track resumes and audited LinkedIn. All four pass the substrate-validator filter.

---

**Capture #10**

*Raw:* Identity markers cost in adjacent lanes. My callsign "RUFIO" reads as community membership and credibility inside the defense lane — operators recognize it. The same callsign in a corporate F500 L&D context reads as performative-military, creates dissonance against an Ed.D. positioning, and costs me some chance at the Deloitte / McKinsey interview. You can't optimize identity markers for both audiences. The honest move is to know what your markers cost where, and choose where you're willing to pay. My math: keep RUFIO, accept the small cost in the 30% bridge lane to preserve the larger return in the 70% defense lane. Everyone with a callsign, religious marker, regional accent, or niche affiliation in their public identity is making this trade — most aren't doing the math.

*Domain:* `OD/Soc` + `Leadership/PD`
*Surface:* `LI-post` or `Newsletter`
*Status:* `captured`
*Hook angle:* "Your identity markers are positioning decisions. Most people aren't doing the math."

---

**Capture #9**

*Raw:* You can run two resumes. You only get one LinkedIn. That asymmetry forces a positioning choice resumes don't. Most career-pivoters discover this too late and end up with a profile that signals neither lane clearly. The strategic move: lean LinkedIn toward your portfolio's majority weighting, and signal the secondary lane through About-section framing, Featured content, and Skills inventory — not through headline real estate. Headlines are one-shot; spend yours on what most of your applications need.

*Domain:* `OD/Soc` + `Meta`
*Surface:* `LI-post` or `LI-carousel`
*Status:* `captured`
*Hook angle:* "The dual-resume / single-LinkedIn problem nobody warns you about."

---

**Capture #8**

*Raw:* When you publicly post on LinkedIn asking for help translating your background, the post becomes part of your profile. Recruiters who later pull you up don't see the wisdom of asking for help — they see someone still figuring it out. The cost of the post isn't the bandwidth of replies; it's the second-order signal that becomes searchable forever. Documentation only works when what you're documenting is forward motion. Asking-for-help posts are anti-documentation.

*Domain:* `Meta` + `Leadership/PD`
*Surface:* `LI-post` or `Newsletter`
*Status:* `captured`
*Hook angle:* "The hidden cost of publicly asking for help."

---

**Capture #7**

*Raw:* The same job title reads as IC at the function level and as managerial at the grade level. "Solutions Architect" reads as individual contributor. "Senior Manager, Solutions Architect" reads as manager-track. The function name is what most people lead with; the grade-level is what most people hide. Hiring managers at primes scan grade before function. Most candidates lose offers they should win because they foregrounded the wrong half of their own title.

*Domain:* `Leadership/PD` + `OD/Soc`
*Surface:* `LI-post`
*Status:* `captured`
*Hook angle:* "Most candidates undersell themselves in the first three words of their resume."

## Session-end review prompt (UPDATED in v0.2)

At the end of a working session — with me, with yourself in a journal, after a podcast recording, after a class — run this prompt:

> *From this session, what was said or realized that an outsider would find interesting precisely because I find it obvious? Anything I caught myself explaining as if it were ordinary that probably isn't? And of those — which would a substrate-validator (someone who's lived inside an institution) recognize the reality of?*

Capture whatever surfaces — don't filter for "is this content-worthy" at the capture stage. That filter is the curse-of-knowledge filter and it kills good captures. The audience filter and harvest filter run downstream, at the harvest review.

## Harvest review cadence

- **Weekly (Sunday):** scan captures from the week. Move 1–3 to `drafted`. Move drafts to `posted` on a sustainable cadence (matches B2 micro guidance: ~3–5 LinkedIn posts/week, 1 newsletter/week, 1 podcast/week, shorts as flow allows).
- **Monthly:** scan the harvest tracker. Which domains are producing ICP-quality engagement? Which aren't? This is the retrospective signal — the tags become the analysis.
- **At ~60 days of capture + post practice:** review against Macro A22 validation (revised v2.7). Either compounding signal is showing (merge-back conditions; this doc archives into macro), or no ICP signal is surfacing on any domain (A22 weakens; revisit DDC fit).

## Merge-back trigger

This doc archives and merges back into macro when Asset 1 compounding is reached per Macro A20 definition:

1. Sustained subscriber/listener/follower growth without proportional production-effort increase.
2. Warm DM inbound from ICP (defense / OD / HR / L&D / consulting buyers, or operator-class experts).
3. Convergent topic signal — one or two domains pulling significantly more weight than the rest in the harvest tracker.

When merged: validated frame becomes canonical macro content; this doc archives to predecessors index.

If frame falsifies instead (60 days of capture + posting produces no ICP signal across any domain): macro A20/A21/A22 update to reflect what broke; this doc archives without merging.
